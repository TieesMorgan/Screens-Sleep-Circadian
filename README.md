# Screens, Sleep & Your Body Clock

**A Digital Wellness Learning Module for Emerging Adults**  
*By Tiees Morgan*

🔗 **Live Site:** [https://tieesmorgan.github.io/Screens-Sleep-Circadian/](https://tieesmorgan.github.io/Screens-Sleep-Circadian/)

## Overview

**Screens, Sleep & Your Body Clock** is a self-paced, interactive educational module (~30 minutes) designed for emerging adults (late teens through late 20s), higher-education administrators, student health centers, and clinicians. Rather than relying on rigid, one-size-fits-all digital detox rules, this module presents **five evidence-based strategies as a stepped, culturally responsive continuum** to protect circadian health, sleep architecture, and next-day cognitive performance.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this module, learners will be able to:
1. **Explain** the photobiological pathway from evening screen light (`450–480 nm`) to the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) and its effects on melatonin suppression, circadian phase delay, sleep architecture, and next-day attention.
2. **Identify** why emerging adults face heightened vulnerability due to self-managed schedules, peer-connection drives (FOMO, digital telepressure), and ongoing prefrontal cortex maturation.
3. **Compare** five stepped intervention strategies alongside their barriers, moderators, and assessment measures.
4. **Adapt** sleep and screen strategies to diverse cultural, socioeconomic, work, and family realities.
5. **Recognize** why supervised Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) skills training outperforms passive information alone (5–7× more effective).

## Module Structure

* **Audio Summary:** Built-in text-to-speech overview with adjustable playback speeds and full transcript.
* **Why Emerging Adults?:** Explores developmental shifts, mental health vulnerabilities, and neurodevelopmental mismatches.
* **The Science:** Breaks down ipRGCs, melanopsin, Dim Light Melatonin Onset (DLMO) shifts, sleep-onset latency, and cognitive impacts with data visualizations.
* **Culture & Context:** Examines caregiving duties, shift work, multigenerational living, and code-switching to replace punitive screen bans with realistic adaptations.
* **Building the Skills:** Applies the five SEL competencies alongside evidence-based modalities (MBSR, CBT techniques) and multimethod assessment frameworks.
* **Five Evidence-Based Strategies:**
  * *Tier 1 — Harm Reduction:* **1. Spectral Display Tuning** & **2. Bedroom Sanctuary**
  * *Tier 2 — Optimal Recovery:* **3. Disconnection Window** & **4. Chronotherapy**
  * *Cross-Tier Foundation:* **5. SEL Skills Training**
* **Check Your Understanding:** Interactive retrieval-practice quiz with immediate feedback.
* **References:** Full academic bibliography grounding all empirical claims.
