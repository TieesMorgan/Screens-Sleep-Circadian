## Overview

**Screens, Sleep & Your Body Clock** is a self-paced, research-backed learning module tailored for **emerging adults (ages 18–29)**, postsecondary wellness programs, and student health services. It frames digital wellness as a flexible continuum balancing light exposure, boundaries, routines, and self-regulation.

## Core Learning Objectives

Learners will be able to:
1. Explain the biological pathway from evening light (`ipRGCs` to `SCN`) and its impact on melatonin and sleep.
2. Analyze how developmental, psychological, and social factors shape evening tech habits.
3. Evaluate the evidence behind five core digital wellness strategies.
4. Compare and justify strategies based on individual needs and barriers.
5. Design a culturally responsive, evidence-based digital wellness plan.

## Module Structure

The interactive site features 11 core sections:
* **01 · Learning Destination:** Objectives, summary, and a 1-minute audio overview.
* **02 · Population:** Focuses on emerging adult autonomy, FOMO, and emotional demands.
* **03 · Research Foundation:** Outlines academic goals and postsecondary applications.
* **04 · Understand the Mechanism:** Explains photobiological pathways and phase-delay models.
* **05 · Digital Wellness Strategies:** Covers spectral display tuning, bedroom sanctuary rules, pre-bed disconnection windows, chronotherapy, and SEL self-regulation.
* **06 · Critical Appraisal:** Critiques study designs and literature (e.g., Conley, 2015).
* **07 · Culture, Context & Feasibility:** Adapts strategies for diverse lifestyles, shift work, and regional light conditions.
* **08 · Skills Across the Lifespan:** An *Identify → Choose → Rehearse → Reflect* practice cycle.
* **09 · Knowledge Check:** A 4-question self-assessment quiz.
* **10 · Frequently Asked Questions:** Addresses blue light, night modes, and tracking.
* **11 · Academic Reference List:** A 20-source bibliography with DOIs.
